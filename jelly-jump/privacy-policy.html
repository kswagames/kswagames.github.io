Privacy Policy for Jelly Jump: Sky Bounce

Effective date: November 24, 2026 (version 1.1 of the app) App: Jelly Jump: Sky Bounce (Android package com.kirk.jellyjump) Developer: KSwag ("we", "us") Contact: kswagames@gmail.com

Summary

  • We, the developer, do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. We don't run servers, accounts, or analytics of our own.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show, measure, and protect those ads (details below).
  • An optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (US$1.99 or local equivalent) is processed by Google Play. Buying it turns off all ads and the ad-related data collection in the app.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, your sound on/off setting, and whether you own "Remove Ads". These stay only on your device. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you haven't bought "Remove Ads", the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show: - a banner on the title and game-over screens, - an occasional full-screen ad between games (at most about once every three games), and - an optional video ad you can choose to watch to continue a game.

According to Google's disclosure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares: - Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device, - IP address: may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location, - Product interactions: for example app launches, taps, and video views, - Diagnostic information: for example app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). We do not receive any of this data in a form that identifies you.

  • Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device). Buying "Remove Ads" stops the app from loading the ads SDK's ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before any ads load, we show Google's consent form, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy options" on the game's title screen.
  • Learn more:
    • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
    • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    • Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
    • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

3. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your name, email, card, or other payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns "Remove Ads", so that it can restore the purchase on reinstall or on a new device. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Permissions

The app uses the Internet and network state permissions to load ads and reach Google Play. It uses the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) for ads, and the billing permission for the purchase. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

5. Children

Jelly Jump is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it (for ad data, also see Google's controls above).

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data collected by Google for ads, use your device's ad settings and Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.

7. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing that version.
