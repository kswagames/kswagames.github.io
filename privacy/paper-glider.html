Who we are

Paper Glider is published by KSWA Games (GitHub account kswagames).

For a privacy question, a deletion request, or anything else about this policy, email kswagames@gmail.com.

The app

Paper Glider is a casual flying game. You throw a paper plane from a rooftop, earn in-game gold based on how far it flies, and spend that gold on plane upgrades. The game is free to play with ads, and offers an optional one-time purchase that removes ads.

This policy covers the Paper Glider Android app. Other KSWA Games apps have their own privacy policies. If you open a website or another app from an ad, that destination has its own policy.

Information the app collects directly

The app does not require an account, sign-in, or registration. KSWA Games does not directly collect personal information through Paper Glider. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Your game progress (gold, upgrade levels, best distance) and whether you bought “Remove Ads” are saved only on your device so the game can be played. They are not sent to KSWA Games.

Advertising and third parties

Paper Glider shows advertisements with the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). On Android, that SDK uses Google Play services. These are the only third parties that process information through the app.

When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Advertising ID. The resettable advertising identifier provided by Android.
  • Device information. Details such as the device model, operating system version, language, and screen size.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address. The app does not ask you for a precise GPS location. Approximate location, when used, comes from the IP address associated with the ad request.
  • App interaction and ad interaction data. Information about your use of the app in connection with ads, and about how you interact with ads, such as views and clicks.
  • Crash and diagnostic data used by the SDK. Technical data the Google Mobile Ads SDK uses to operate, diagnose problems, and protect the ad service.

KSWA Games does not receive this advertising data on its own servers. Google processes it under Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Google may process this information on servers in the United States and in other countries where Google operates, as described in those policies. KSWA Games is responsible for Paper Glider and for this policy. Google is responsible for information it collects through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

How that information is used

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is used to:

  • Show ads in the app
  • Measure ads, including views, clicks, and ad performance
  • Help prevent fraud, invalid traffic, and abuse

KSWA Games does not sell your personal information. Some US state privacy laws treat the use of advertising data for personalized ads as sharing or targeted advertising. You can refuse or limit that use with the choices below.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

Paper Glider offers an optional one-time purchase, “Remove Ads”, sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment. KSWA Games never receives or stores your card or other payment details.

The app asks Google Play only whether you own the “Remove Ads” product, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new device. Google’s handling of purchase information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. After you buy “Remove Ads”, the app stops requesting ads.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and US states

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state that requires a choice about personalized advertising, Paper Glider shows Google’s consent message before personalized ads are requested. The message is provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

The message describes Google’s use of information for ads and lets you allow, refuse, or manage options, including whether ads may be personalized. Where a choice is required, Paper Glider uses the choice you make in that message.

You can change your choice later in any of these ways:

  1. Use the privacy options in Google’s consent message when it is on screen. If Paper Glider offers the privacy-options entry from Google’s User Messaging Platform, open it and update your choice there.
  2. Clear the choice stored on your device so the message can be shown again. On Android, open Settings, tap Apps, tap Paper Glider, tap Storage or Storage & cache, then tap Clear data or Clear storage. Open Paper Glider again. This removes the app’s local data on your device, including the saved consent choice. It does not delete information Google has already collected.
  3. Limit personalized ads at the device level, as described in the next section.
  4. If you are signed in to a Google account, review ad personalization in Google My Ad Center.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can opt out of personalized ads, and reset or delete your advertising ID, in Android settings. Menu names differ by Android version and by the company that made your phone. If you cannot find an item below, open Settings and search for “Ads”.

Android 12 and later.

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Security & privacy, Privacy, or Security and privacy. You may also need More privacy settings or Other privacy settings.
  3. Tap Ads.
  4. Tap Delete advertising ID and confirm. On some versions the control is labeled as opting out of interest-based or personalized ads.

A common path is Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Older Android versions.

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Google, then Ads.
  3. Turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording may be “Opt out of interest-based ads”).
  4. To replace the current identifier, tap Reset advertising ID.

Deleting or resetting the advertising ID stops apps and advertising partners from using the previous ID to profile that device. You may still see ads. After you opt out or delete the ID, ads are less personalized or not personalized, depending on your consent choice and on Google’s handling of the request.

Retention and deletion

KSWA Games does not operate accounts for Paper Glider and does not store personal information from the app on its own servers. There is no Paper Glider profile on our systems to keep.

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained by Google for the periods described in Google’s privacy policy. KSWA Games cannot delete Google’s copies itself. You can manage ad data Google holds through the Android controls above and through Google My Ad Center.

If you believe we hold personal information about you and you want it deleted, email kswagames@gmail.com with the subject “Paper Glider deletion request”. Tell us how to recognize the information. We will delete personal information we hold about you, unless the law requires us to keep it, and we will reply to confirm what we did. If the information is held only by Google, we will say so and point you to Google’s tools.

Children

Paper Glider is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, email kswagames@gmail.com. Please include enough detail for us to find the information. We will delete personal information we hold about that child. You can also use the Android advertising controls on the child’s device, and Google’s privacy tools, for information held by Google.

Security

Paper Glider does not use an account or a KSWA Games server that stores personal information. Ad requests are sent by Google’s SDK over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information processed by Google or stored on your device.

This policy is effective November 24, 2026.

If you continue to use Paper Glider after an update, the revised policy applies to that use. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the app. Uninstalling Paper Glider stops it from requesting ads on your device.
